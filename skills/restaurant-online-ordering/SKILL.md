# Commission-free restaurant online ordering

## Why direct ordering
Aggregators charge restaurants roughly 15–30% per order and keep the customer relationship. A direct channel has a fixed software cost and you own customer data for re-marketing. Aggregators still help discovery — the usual strategy is **both**: use aggregators to be found, move regulars to your own channel.

## Quick math to show the user
Monthly aggregator commission = orders × AOV × commission %.
Example: 600 orders × $25 × 25% = **$3,750/month**.
Direct channel cost = software subscription + payment fees + delivery cost (own driver or courier) + marketing.
Ask for their real numbers; don't promise savings without them.

## Setup steps
1. Ordering website with your domain; menu with photos and add-ons (`hyperzod-catalog-builder`).
2. Pickup first (simplest), then delivery: own driver(s), third-party courier, or both. Set radius/zones, fees, minimum order.
3. Payments: card + wallets; cash on delivery if common locally.
4. Order handling: merchant app/tablet alerts, thermal printer, prep times.
5. Drive traffic: Google Business Profile "Order online" link, website button, QR codes on tables/packaging/flyers inserted in aggregator bags (check aggregator terms), social bio links.
6. Retention: loyalty points, first-direct-order coupon, membership free delivery, push and email.
7. Branded app later, once repeat customers exist.
