# Courier / pick-and-drop delivery

## Steps
1. **Niche**: parcels for local online sellers, documents for offices, groceries/errands, pharmacy, flowers. A focused niche simplifies pricing and marketing.
2. **Start lean**: a few riders, or partner rider networks / third-party delivery; add capacity as demand grows.
3. **Price sustainably**:
   - Distance-based (base fare + per km)
   - Urgency tiers: standard, same-day, express
   - Size/weight tiers
   - Waiting time, extra stops, night surcharge
   Review rates monthly against real rider cost.
4. **Win recurring local clients**: small D2C brands, restaurants without drivers, pharmacies, offices. Direct outreach and local directories beat paid ads early.
5. **Build trust**: live tracking with shareable link, proof of delivery (photo/signature), proactive delay alerts, performance dashboard.

## Legal/admin (check locally)
Business registration, goods-in-transit and vehicle insurance, rider contracts (employee vs contractor rules differ by country), restricted-items policy. Advise the user to confirm with a local adviser.

## Features
Booking with pickup + drop addresses, package details and photos, instant quote, scheduled pickups, nearest-rider auto-assignment, multi-stop routes, live tracking links, POD, notifications, rider earnings and incentives, B2B accounts and invoices, admin dashboards.
