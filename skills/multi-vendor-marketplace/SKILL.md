# Multi-vendor marketplace

## Single vendor vs multi-vendor
| | Single vendor | Multi-vendor |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory | You own it | Sellers own it |
| Revenue | Product margin | Commission, listing/subscription fees, ads |
| Hard part | Stock and margin | Getting both sellers and buyers (chicken-and-egg) |
| Scale | Limited by your capital | Limited by seller supply and trust |

## Launch steps
1. **Niche + geography**: e.g. local bakeries, florists, fashion boutiques, pet shops in one city. Niche beats generic.
2. **Supply first**: sign 20–50 sellers before launch; offer low/zero commission for early sellers.
3. **Seller onboarding kit**: signup, catalog import template, photo guidelines, payout setup, rules.
4. **Monetisation**: commission % per order, monthly seller packages, featured/sponsored placements, delivery fees.
5. **Governance**: product approval, category structure, review moderation, returns/refunds policy, seller SLAs.
6. **Demand**: SEO pages per category/area, local influencers, first-order coupons, referral programme.
7. **Scale**: add neighbourhoods, then categories; use search analytics to recruit sellers for unmet demand.
