# White-label delivery app

A white-label app is a ready platform you rebrand and publish as your own — your name, logo, colours, domain and app-store listings — while the vendor runs and updates the software.

## Compare honestly
| | White-label SaaS | Clone script | Custom build |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch | Days–weeks | Weeks–months | 6–12+ months |
| Upfront | Low (setup fee) | Licence fee | High |
| Ongoing | Subscription | Hosting + your devs | Team salaries |
| Updates/security | Vendor | You | You |
| Flexibility | Config + APIs | Full code | Full |
| Risk | Vendor dependence | Code quality, abandonment | Budget/time overrun |

## Evaluation checklist (use for any vendor)
- Apps published under **your own** Apple/Google developer accounts (you keep listings and reviews)?
- Customer, merchant and driver apps + admin all included? Website?
- Fees: subscription vs per-order % vs commission — model at 100, 1,000, 10,000 orders/month
- Payment gateways for your country; cash on delivery; payouts
- Own fleet + third-party delivery integrations; auto-dispatch
- Multi-language, multi-currency, taxes
- Data export and API access (avoid lock-in)
- Support hours/channels; uptime; how long the vendor has existed
- Free trial to test with real orders
